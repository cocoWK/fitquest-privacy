FitQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

FitQuest ("we", "our", "the App") is a daily gamified home workout app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. By using FitQuest, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Workout activity data. To provide core features, the App stores your workout activity on your device, including: completed workout records, streak (consecutive days) counts, and total training minutes.
  • Subscription status. If you purchase a FitQuest Pro subscription, the App stores a local indicator that your subscription is active so it can unlock Pro features offline.

2. How Data Is Stored

All workout activity data is stored locally on your device using Android's private app storage (SharedPreferences). The data does not leave your device, is not uploaded to any server, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect sensitive health data such as heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose, or sleep measurements. FitQuest does not use any body sensors.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.
  • We do not require an account or registration.

4. Advertising

FitQuest is a free app that displays advertising served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve ads and measure ad performance, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) or via Google's Ads Settings.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

FitQuest Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card or payment information. All payments are handled by Google according to its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing and Selling of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law or as described in this policy (e.g., advertising partners processing data for their own purposes as described in Section 4).

7. Children's Privacy

FitQuest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Security

Your data is protected by the Android application sandbox, which isolates app data from other apps and from other users of the device. We do not transmit your data over the network.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
